Support

NIYYAH is a Qur'an learning companion for reading, understanding, reflection, and shared study. We welcome questions and respectful corrections.

Get help

For help with your account, sign-in, circles, audio, or app navigation, email niyyahhapp@gmail.com.

Report a content concern

For a possible typo, citation issue, translation concern, recitation issue, or source question, include the surah and ayah if you can. We take Qur'anic-content reports seriously.

Delete your account or local data

In NIYYAH, open your profile and choose “Delete Account & Local Data.” For additional help, email us from the address connected to your account.
