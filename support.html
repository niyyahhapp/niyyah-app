NIYYAH QURAN COMPANION

How can we help?

NIYYAH is designed to help you read, understand, reflect, and build a consistent relationship with the Qur'an. If something is not working as expected, we're here to help.

Contact support

For account access, app issues, audio problems, content concerns, or other support questions, email us and include the app version and a short description of the issue.

Email NIYYAH Support

Common help

  • Account access: If you cannot sign in, sign out and try again. If the problem continues, contact support.
  • Qur'an reading: If Arabic text, translation, or a surah does not display correctly, tell us the surah and ayah.
  • Recitation: If audio will not start, stop, or resume, include the surah and ayah where the issue occurs.
  • Reflections and journal: Your saved reflections are private within the app. Do not send private journal or dua text in a support email unless you intentionally choose to share it.
  • Progress: If your progress does not appear as expected, include the screen and action that led to the problem.
  • Content concerns: For a translation, attribution, source, or Qur'an text concern, identify the surah and ayah so we can investigate it carefully.

Account and privacy

You can manage your account from within NIYYAH. Account deletion is available in the app. Read our Privacy Policy for information about data and privacy.

Need to report a problem?

Please contact support with enough detail for us to reproduce the issue. A useful report includes the screen, the action you took, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
