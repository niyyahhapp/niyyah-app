Contact support

Need help with NIYYAH? Contact us about account access, Qur'an reading, recitation, reflections, progress, content concerns, or anything that is not working as expected.

When contacting us, include:

  • The screen where the issue occurred
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • The surah and ayah, if the issue concerns Qur'an content or audio

Please do not include private journal entries, duas, or personal notes unless you intentionally choose to share them.
