Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Overview

NIYYAH is a Qur'an understanding and reflection app designed to help users read, understand, reflect, and journal one ayah at a time. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information Stored In The App

NIYYAH stores learning data on your device, including reflections, duas, favorite ayahs, vocabulary words, memorization status, completed ayahs, gentle streak data, circle data, and profile data.

Account And Login

NIYYAH may offer Google or Apple sign-in for account access and future sync. When sign-in is active, the app may receive basic account information such as your email address, name, and profile identifier from the login provider. We do not ask for passwords from Google or Apple accounts.

Waitlist Information

If you choose to join the NIYYAH waitlist, your email address is processed by our email or waitlist provider so we can send product and launch updates. You can unsubscribe using the link in those emails.

Religious Content

NIYYAH displays Qur'an text, translation, source tafsir, and learning notes. Qur'an Arabic text should not be generated, paraphrased, or altered by the app. NIYYAH is not a scholar, mufti, fatwa service, or replacement for qualified Islamic teachers.

Payments

No paid digital features are currently enabled in NIYYAH. If paid app features are introduced later, this privacy policy will be updated before they become available.

Data Sharing

NIYYAH does not sell personal data. We may use trusted service providers for email, waitlist, authentication, hosting, or app analytics if those services are added. We only use providers needed to operate and improve the app.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete local NIYYAH data inside the app profile screen using “Delete Account & Local Data.” This removes the local profile, journal, progress, favorites, vocabulary, circles, and onboarding state from that device. See the Account Deletion page for details.

Children And Youth

NIYYAH is designed to be beginner-friendly and youth-safe, but it is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children. If a parent, guardian, school, or organization uses NIYYAH with younger users, they should supervise use according to their own policies and local laws.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to niyyahhapp@gmail.com.
