Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Overview

NIYYAH is a Qur'an understanding and reflection app designed to help users read, understand, reflect, and journal one ayah at a time. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information Stored In The App

NIYYAH stores learning data on your device, including reflections, duas, favorite ayahs, vocabulary words, completed ayahs, gentle streak data, circle data, and profile data. Reflections, duas, journal entries, and personal Qur'an notes remain on your device.

Account And Login

NIYYAH offers Google and Apple sign-in. When you sign in, NIYYAH receives the account email address and unique identifier provided by the sign-in provider, along with the display name you choose. This information is used to authenticate you and provide private Circle sync. NIYYAH never asks for your Google or Apple password.

Waitlist Information

If you choose to join the NIYYAH waitlist, your email address is processed by our email or waitlist provider so we can send product and launch updates. You can unsubscribe using the link in those emails.

Religious Content

NIYYAH displays Qur'an text, translation, source tafsir, and learning notes. The Arabic Qur'an text is never generated, paraphrased, or altered by AI. NIYYAH is not a scholar, mufti, fatwa service, or replacement for qualified Islamic teachers.

Payments

NIYYAH is currently free and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Data Sharing

NIYYAH does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. NIYYAH uses Supabase to authenticate signed-in users and sync private Circle data. NIYYAH may use an email or waitlist provider to send requested updates. No third-party analytics SDK is enabled in the app.

Deleting Your Data

When signed in, open your profile and choose “Request Account Deletion” to submit an authenticated account-deletion request. Signing out removes the protected sign-in session from that device. Local reflections, progress, vocabulary, and Circle data remain on the device until the app is deleted. See the Account Deletion page for details.

Children And Youth

NIYYAH is designed to be beginner-friendly and youth-safe, but it is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children. If a parent, guardian, school, or organization uses NIYYAH with younger users, they should supervise use according to their own policies and local laws.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to niyyahhapp@gmail.com.
