About NIYYAH

NIYYAH is a Qur'an understanding and reflection app. It helps users read, understand, reflect, journal, review vocabulary, track progress, and learn in a calm and structured way.

Educational Purpose

NIYYAH is an educational and reflection tool. It is not a scholar, mufti, fatwa service, religious authority, therapist, medical provider, legal advisor, or emergency service.

Qur'an Text, Translation, And Tafsir

NIYYAH does not intentionally alter Arabic Qur'an text. Translations, tafsir summaries, and learning notes are provided for understanding and reflection. If you notice an issue with Qur'anic text, pronunciation, translation, tafsir, or source attribution, please contact us immediately.

No Religious Rulings

NIYYAH does not provide fatwas, personal religious rulings, marriage advice, political guidance, medical advice, or legal advice. Users should ask qualified scholars or trusted teachers for religious questions requiring authority or personal judgment.

User Reflections

Users may write private reflections, actions, and duas in the app. These entries are stored locally on the user's device unless optional account sync is enabled.

Accounts And Deletion

Users who are signed in can open the profile screen and choose “Request Account Deletion.” Local learning data remains on the device until the app is deleted. You can also review the Account Deletion page.

Payments

NIYYAH is currently free and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Acceptable Use

Do not misuse NIYYAH, attempt to interfere with its operation, upload harmful content, or use the app to harass others. Circle features should be used with sincerity, privacy, and respect.

No Guarantee

NIYYAH is provided as-is. We aim to build a thoughtful, accurate, and safe product, but we cannot promise the app will always be error-free or available.
