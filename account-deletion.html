Account Deletion

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Request Account Deletion

Open NIYYAH, go to your profile, and tap “Request Account Deletion.” You must be signed in so NIYYAH can identify the account associated with the request.

Delete Waitlist Email

If you joined the waitlist, use the unsubscribe link in any NIYYAH email. You can also email us and ask for your waitlist email to be removed.

What Happens Next

The app records an authenticated deletion request and signs you out. We then process the request and delete or anonymize account data that is no longer needed, except for limited records that must be retained for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

Local Data On Your Device

Reflections, duas, progress, vocabulary, and other private learning data are stored locally on your device. Delete the NIYYAH app to remove that local data from the device.

Support Backup

If the in-app deletion flow is unavailable, email us from the same email address used for your NIYYAH account and ask for help with account deletion.
