Sources & Trust

At NIYYAH, trust comes before features. When verified source material is available, we cite it. When it is not available, we would rather leave it out than present unsourced religious content.

Our Commitment

NIYYAH is designed to help people begin understanding the Qur'an. It is not a replacement for scholars, teachers, or the study of authentic tafsir.

Qur'an Text

Arabic Qur'an text comes from established, authenticated digital Qur'an datasets. The source used by NIYYAH is listed within the app. The Qur'an text itself is never generated, rewritten, or modified by AI.

Translation

Translations are displayed with clear source attribution. Translation sources are listed within the app.

Recitation

Audio uses recordings from verified reciters. NIYYAH does not use AI-generated Qur'an recitation.

Source Tafsir

Source tafsir is displayed only where verified, attributed source material has been added. We would rather omit source tafsir than display unsourced or AI-generated religious explanations.

Educational Content

Learning summaries, reflection prompts, vocabulary, quizzes, and other educational tools are designed to support study. These features are clearly distinguished from source tafsir and should not be treated as scholarly rulings or religious verdicts.
